1. Introduction and Scope

The Legends Lab develops and publishes software applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information across all our products and constitutes a company-wide umbrella policy.

Because our applications differ significantly in how they operate, the specific data practices of each application are described in a separate App-Specific Privacy Notice. That notice is published and maintained as an independent document alongside this Policy. This Policy does not enumerate individual applications, as new applications are released over time.

Where an App-Specific Privacy Notice conflicts with this Policy for a particular application, the App-Specific Notice controls for that application only. For any application for which no App-Specific Notice has been published, this Policy applies in full.

This Policy does not apply to:

  • Third-party services, APIs, platforms, or app stores that our applications may connect to or be distributed through;
  • Information you provide directly to third parties (e.g., Google Play, Apple App Store);
  • Any external website linked from our applications.

By installing, accessing, or using any of our applications, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy and any applicable App-Specific Notice.

2. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • "Offline Standalone Application" means an application designed to operate entirely on the user's device without transmitting data to us or to third parties.
  • "Connected/API Application" means an application that transmits data over the internet to provide functionality.
  • "Ephemeral Data" means technical data processed in transit solely to fulfill a request and not retained beyond that purpose.
  • "Support Data" means information you voluntarily provide when contacting us (e.g., email address, message content).
  • "App-Specific Privacy Notice" means the privacy notice published separately for a particular application.
  • "Services" means all applications, software, websites, and services published by The Legends Lab.

3. Data Minimization Principle

We design our applications on a data-minimization basis: we collect only what is strictly necessary to deliver the requested functionality. Where an application can operate entirely offline, we build it to do so. Where connectivity is required, we limit transmission to what is technically necessary.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Offline Standalone Applications

For applications that operate fully offline:

  • We do not collect, view, log, monitor, transmit, store, or query any Personal Data, usage telemetry, or local media assets.
  • All processing occurs on your device. Any data generated remains under your control.
  • Uninstalling the application removes locally stored data, subject to your device's backup settings.

4.2 Connected/API Applications

For applications that require internet connectivity, we may process:

  • Technical request data: IP address, device type, operating system version, app version, and request parameters necessary to fulfill the feature;
  • Diagnostic data (only if explicitly enabled): crash reports and error logs, anonymized where feasible.

This data is ephemeral unless stated otherwise in the relevant App-Specific Privacy Notice. We do not use it for profiling, advertising, or sale.

4.3 Support Communications

If you contact us, we collect your email address and the content of your message. We use this solely to respond to and resolve your request.

4.4 Information We Do Not Collect

Unless an App-Specific Privacy Notice expressly states otherwise, we do not collect:

  • Precise geolocation;
  • Contacts, calendars, or SMS content;
  • Biometric data;
  • Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking;
  • Financial account details (payments are handled by app stores or payment processors).

5. Device Permissions

Our applications request only the permissions necessary for their stated functionality. Each permission is explained at the point of request and in the relevant App-Specific Privacy Notice. You may deny or revoke permissions through your device settings; doing so may disable certain features. Denying a permission does not affect your use of features that do not require it.

6. Third-Party Services

Some applications integrate third-party APIs or services (for example, content delivery, authentication, or payment processors). Where we do so:

  • We require such providers to maintain reasonable security standards;
  • We do not control their independent data practices, and their own privacy policies apply;
  • The relevant App-Specific Privacy Notice lists the third-party services used by that application;
  • You are encouraged to review the privacy policies of any third-party service before use.

We do not lease, sell, rent, or share Personal Data with advertising aggregators, data brokers, or marketing platforms.

7. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide the application and its features;
  • Legitimate interests: to maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve reliability;
  • Consent: where required (e.g., optional diagnostics or analytics), which you may withdraw at any time;
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law.

8. Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period
Support communications Up to 12 months after resolution, then deleted
Ephemeral request data Not retained beyond request fulfillment
Crash/diagnostic logs (if enabled) Up to 90 days, then deleted or anonymized
Offline app data Stored only on your device, controlled by you

We may retain data longer where required by law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for connected applications, secure development practices, and access controls on any internal systems.

However, no method of transmission, storage, or electronic processing is completely secure. You acknowledge and agree that:

  • We cannot guarantee absolute security of any data;
  • You provide any information at your own risk;
  • Our liability in the event of any security incident is governed exclusively by the Limitation of Liability clause in our Terms of Service, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law;
  • Where we are legally required to notify you of a data breach, we will do so within the timeframe required by applicable law (e.g., 72 hours under GDPR, or as required under other jurisdictions).

10. International Data Transfers

Where data is transferred outside your country of residence, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place consistent with applicable law (e.g., standard contractual clauses where required).

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the Personal Data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data;
  • Request deletion ("right to be forgotten");
  • Object to or restrict processing;
  • Data portability;
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is consent-based);
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority;
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

To exercise these rights, contact ali.legends.lab.company@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (typically 30 days for GDPR, 45 days for CCPA). We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling the request.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what Personal Data we collect, the right to delete, the right to correct, and the right to opt out of sale (note: we do not sell Personal Data). We do not discriminate against users for exercising CCPA rights.

12. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) unless an App-Specific Privacy Notice expressly states otherwise.

  • We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children below the applicable age.
  • If an application is intended for children, the App-Specific Privacy Notice will describe compliance with applicable children's privacy laws (e.g., COPPA in the U.S., GDPR-K in the EEA, or equivalent in your jurisdiction).

If you believe a child has provided us Personal Data, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

13. Cookies and Tracking (Websites)

Our websites may use strictly necessary cookies. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on our own properties. Where analytics are used, they are configured to minimize data collection and, where required, are consent-based (e.g., cookie banners for EEA users).

14. Professional and Sensitive Data

We do not request or process special categories of Personal Data (health, religious beliefs, biometric data, etc.). You should not submit such information to us through support communications.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date and, where appropriate, additional notice via in-app notification or email. Continued use of our applications after changes means you accept the revised Policy.

This Policy intentionally does not list individual applications. New applications released by The Legends Lab are automatically covered by this Policy and, where applicable, by their own separate App-Specific Privacy Notice.

16. App-Specific Privacy Notices

Each application published by The Legends Lab may have its own App-Specific Privacy Notice, published as a separate document. To find the applicable notice, refer to the privacy policy link provided on the application's store listing page or within the application itself.

Where an application does not have a separate App-Specific Privacy Notice, this Policy applies to that application in full.
