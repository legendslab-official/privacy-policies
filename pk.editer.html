App-Specific Privacy Policy: PK.Editor

Application PK.Editor Effective Date 16-11-2026
Entity The Legends Lab Company Contact Email ali.legends.lab.company@gmail.com

This App-Specific Privacy Policy applies exclusively to PK.Editor (the "App"). It supplements and should be read together with The Legends Lab Company's Master Privacy Policy, available at https://legendslab-official.github.io/privacy-policies/. In case of any conflict regarding this App, this document controls.

1. Information We Collect

We follow strict data minimization. We only collect what is necessary for the App to function.

1.1 Free Users — No Account Required

  • You can use PK.Editor's core editing features without creating an account or logging in.
  • For free users, we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.
  • Your videos, images, audio, and project files are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

1.2 Pro Users — Optional Account & Subscription

If you choose to purchase a Pro subscription, you must create an account or log in. In this case, we collect and store in our secure database:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if provided during registration)
  • Your subscription status (active/inactive) and plan details

This information is used solely for account management, subscription validation, and customer support. It is not used for advertising or resale.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see Section 10).

1.3 Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party AI APIs

Certain optional AI features require transmission of media to third-party API providers:

  • Auto Captions: When you use this feature, the audio portion of your video is sent to a third-party speech-to-text API. This data is ephemeral and not retained by us after the request is fulfilled.
  • Background Removal: When you use this feature, the image or video frame is sent to a third-party background segmentation API. This data is also ephemeral and not retained.

1.4 Information Collected Automatically via Firebase Crashlytics

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to monitor App stability. Firebase may automatically collect basic technical data such as device type, OS version, and crash logs. This data is used solely to fix bugs and improve performance.

1.5 Payment Information

All subscription payments are processed entirely by the third-party App Store or Payment Gateway. We do not collect, view, or store your card details, bank information, or full transaction IDs. We only receive a confirmation status from the gateway that your payment was successful, along with your subscription plan and validity period.

1.6 Advertising and Ad SDKs

The App may display advertisements, including before, during, or after the export process. We may use third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, and advertising partners to display these ads.

Third-party advertising partners may automatically collect certain information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (such as Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where applicable)
  • IP address
  • Device type, operating system version, app version, and language
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data, such as impressions, clicks, and viewability

We do not provide your videos, images, audio, or project files to advertising partners. We do not sell your personal data.

Where required by law, we obtain consent before using your data for personalized advertising. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier and manage personalized ads through your device settings. If you purchase a Pro subscription, ads may still be shown unless the App expressly states that your plan is ad-free.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • For Free Users: To deliver core editing features locally on your device. No personal data is processed on our servers.
  • For Pro Users: To create and manage your account, validate your subscription, provide customer support, and communicate important service updates.
  • AI Feature Processing: To process and deliver the AI features you request (Auto Captions, Background Removal) via third-party APIs.
  • App Stability & Diagnostics: To monitor App performance, fix crashes, and improve stability via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising: To display ads in the App, including during export, and to measure ad performance through third-party advertising partners.

We do not use your media files for advertising, profiling, or resale.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Media: Your videos, images, and projects remain on your device and are under your control. Deleting the App removes them from your device.
  • Free Users: No personal data is retained on our servers.
  • Pro Users: Account data (name, email, phone, subscription status) is retained for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion request, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., payment records for tax/accounting purposes may be retained per legal requirements).
  • Ephemeral API Data: Audio/image data sent for Auto Captions or Background Removal is not retained by us after the request is fulfilled.
  • Crash Logs: Crash logs are retained for 90 days and then automatically deleted or anonymized.
  • Advertising Data: Ad-related data is processed by third-party advertising partners under their own retention policies. We may receive only aggregated or non-identifying ad performance reports.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before being securely deleted.

5. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. You may also:

  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertising where consent is required.
  • Request account deletion by contacting us.

To exercise these rights, contact us at ali.legends.lab.company@gmail.com.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers, including crash reporting, authentication, payment, AI, and advertising partners, may process data in countries other than Pakistan. Where required, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

8. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. We do not serve personalized advertising to children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new effective date. If changes are material, we will provide notice in the App or through other appropriate means.
