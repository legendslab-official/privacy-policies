1. 100% OFFLINE & LOCAL STORAGE

MAPlayer operates strictly on-device. All video files, audio tracks, rendering operations, and subtitle processing execute locally within your device's sandbox hardware environment. No media cache or dynamic content is processed outside your active local storage boundary.

2. NO TELEMETRY OR TRACKING

We believe your media consumption habits are strictly your own private business:

  • ZERO Telemetry: We do not log, capture, audit, or report how you use the application, what interface features you tap, or what timeline sequences you process.
  • ZERO Tracking: No unique device identifiers, tracking SDKs, analytics endpoints, or behavioral tracking cookies are embedded within this code architecture.
  • ZERO Cloud Uploads: Your media files, playlists, and associated metadata never leave your physical device under any operations.

3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS & CORE USE

The application may explicitly require standard system storage permissions (such as Access to Media Files / Photos / Videos / External Volume) solely to locate, open, index, and play your local videos and external subtitle packages. This data is purely read-only for local rendering and is never transmitted externally or exposed to third parties.
